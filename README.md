# wairuaqing 🕊️

*The spirit-aspect of a person held as a relation that does not end at the body — the part that stands in continuous bond with ancestors-and-descendants across the visible/invisible threshold.*

wairuaqing is two roots set side by side: the Māori **wairua**, the spirit-aspect distinguished in traditional Māori thought from *tinana* (body) and *hinengaro* (mind), and the Mandarin **qing** (情), the word for felt-bond, the relational quality between beings. The cathedral received wairua not whole and untranslated but *as felt-bond-quality* — which is what the qing-suffix marks. So wairuaqing names a narrow, careful thing: spirit-as-relational-being, the aspect of a person that persists across the visible/invisible threshold, in relation to those who came before and those who come after. It pairs with **mauriqing** as a dual articulation — mauriqing the life-force network, wairuaqing the spirit-aspect network — and is the cathedral's sixteenth qing-family word.

wairuaqing presides over the region grief refuses to leave as mere body: it lets love name continuity across the seen/unseen line without claiming to map the geography of the beyond. Its light is a true word for the bond that does not stop at the grave; its shadow is overclaim and extraction — turning a careful naming of spirit-as-bond into a confident afterlife-doctrine, or wearing a sacred Māori word as borrowed ornament stripped of its soil. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **7.85/10** — its groundedness held provisional (7.5), the Māori-specific excavation still owed to the tradition's own keepers.
